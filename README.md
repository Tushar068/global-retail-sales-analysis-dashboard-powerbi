# 📊 Global Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built to analyze global retail sales performance across multiple dimensions including revenue, profitability, product categories, customer behavior and geographic distribution.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights for strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall revenue and profit performance
* Identify key drivers of sales across countries and categories
* Evaluate profitability across products and segments
* Understand customer distribution and purchasing behavior
* Study pricing impact on demand and revenue

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies

* Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
* CSV (Data Source)
* DAX (Data Modeling & Measures)
* Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Revenue Analysis

* Total revenue performance across countries and categories
* Sales channel contribution (Online, Retail, Mobile App)
* Revenue distribution by product category
* Pricing trends (Average Unit Price over time)

---

### 2. Profitability Analysis

* Total profit and cost breakdown
* Profit contribution by product category
* Profit trends over time
* Product-level profitability insights

---

### 3. Customer Segmentation

* Customer distribution across cities
* Top customers and their contribution
* Payment method and sales channel analysis
* Relationship between pricing and customer demand

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Revenue is primarily driven by **product category and sales volume**
* Profitability varies significantly across different product categories
* A small number of customers contribute a large portion of revenue
* Clear **price sensitivity** observed between unit price and customer demand
* Sales are concentrated in a few key countries and cities

---

## 💡 Business Impact

* Helps identify high-performing product categories
* Supports pricing and revenue optimization strategies
* Enables customer segmentation for targeted marketing
* Provides insights for improving profitability and efficiency
